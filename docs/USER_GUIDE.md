# User Guide

## Navigating the App
The application is divided into horizontal tabs: Home, Exclusive Jobs, Resume Maker, Job Search Map, and Resume Match.

## Resume Maker
Fill out your personal details, education, experience, and projects. Click **Generate Resume with AI**. The AI will rewrite your inputs into a professional format. You can then print or save it as a PDF.

## Job Search Map
Enter a role and a location. You can also provide permission for the browser to use your GPS location. Specify a radius (e.g., 25km). The map will populate with pins representing jobs scraped from real job boards. Click a pin to view the job details and apply externally.

## Resume Match & Analysis
Upload your existing PDF resume and paste a job description. The AI will analyze the text, provide an estimated ATS readiness score, identify missing keywords, and suggest improvements.
